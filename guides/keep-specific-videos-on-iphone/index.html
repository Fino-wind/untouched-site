How to keep specific videos on your iPhone with Optimize iPhone Storage on

Updated September 30, 2026

Short answer

You can’t do it natively inside the Photos app. Optimize iPhone Storage applies to your whole library; iOS has no setting that exempts one video, one album or your Favorites. When switching to "Download and Keep Originals" is impossible due to limited iPhone storage, the most reliable way to keep specific videos playable instantly and offline is storing their unmodified originals outside the Photos library container—either manually via Share › Export Unmodified Original to the Files app (On My iPhone), or with live download feedback, paired Live Photo retention, and SHA-256 verification using Untouched. The rest of your library stays optimized in iCloud, so you keep the space you freed.

Why there’s no per-video switch

Optimize iPhone Storage is one setting for the whole library. With it on, your iPhone decides which originals to replace with smaller versions based on how much space it needs, and Apple offers no way to pin particular items. Anything inside the Photos library is subject to that decision, so the only way to guarantee a video stays playable offline is a copy that isn’t part of the library.

Option 1: Turn off optimization for everything

Choose Download and Keep Originals in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Photos (in iOS 27, then iPhone Storage). Every video plays instantly again.

Trade-off: your iPhone needs room for your entire library. If you turned on Optimize because a 150 GB library had filled a 256 GB phone, it won’t fit back.

The Common Pitfall: The “Tap Edit” Trick

Many users attempt to force a download by opening a video, tapping Edit, waiting for the high-resolution file to download, and tapping Cancel.

Why it fails: Tapping Edit only grants a temporary lease. Because the video remains inside the Photos system cache, iOS background cache management monitors device storage and silently evicts that file back to iCloud within days or as soon as storage runs tight. You cannot rely on this for travel or offline access.

Option 2: Export the videos to the Files app yourself

  1. In Photos, select the videos you want to keep on the phone.
  2. Tap Share, then Export Unmodified Original.
  3. Choose a folder under On My iPhone and tap Save.
  4. From then on, play them from the Files app.

If a video’s original is only in iCloud, it downloads first. For edited items, Apple notes that the unmodified original is exported.

Trade-offs: while free and built-in, manual export has four major friction points: (1) downloading multiple large 4K videos through the system ShareSheet frequently freezes or silently cancels with zero progress feedback; (2) videos land as bare filenames like IMG_4712.MOV with no continuous gallery swipe-to-play; (3) Live Photos lose interactive touch playback by splitting into separate still and video files; and (4) items in a Face ID-locked Hidden album cannot be exported until you turn off protection in Settings.

Option 3: Use an app that keeps them for you (Untouched)

Untouched does option 2’s job with the gaps filled in. You pick videos in the usual photo picker. For each one it downloads the original once with a live progress bar, then keeps every file Photos holds for the item (a Live Photo’s video, an edited clip’s unedited original) in one readable folder in Files › On My iPhone › Untouched. You play them in Untouched or Files, instantly and offline. It records a SHA-256 checksum for every file so you can verify it later, shows whether each item’s original is still on the device in Photos, and never modifies your library.

Trade-offs: it uses space for what you keep; one item is free and keeping more is paid; and it isn’t a backup. Deleting the app deletes its folder.

Common questions

Can I watch 4K videos on an airplane without Wi-Fi if Optimize Storage is on?

Not if they are only stored in iCloud. When you turn on Airplane Mode or board a flight without Wi-Fi, optimized videos cannot be downloaded. Keep the specific videos you want to watch stored outside the library in Files or Untouched before your flight.

Does adding a video to Favorites force it to stay downloaded?

No. Favorites is only an album tag inside Photos. iOS still offloads favorited videos to iCloud when local storage runs low, leaving low-res thumbnails that require a download to play.

Why does exporting videos to the Files app sometimes freeze or fail?

Selecting multiple 4K videos in Photos and tapping Share › Export Unmodified Original forces the system to download gigabytes in the background without a progress meter. If memory runs low or Wi-Fi drops, the export silently cancels or freezes.

Why not just use the Files app to store videos?

While free, the Files app lists videos as cryptic names like IMG_4712.MOV with no visual timeline grid. You cannot swipe horizontally to the next video like in a photo album, and Live Photos lose their motion. Untouched gives you a native photo-album experience with thumbnail previews and continuous swipe-to-play.
