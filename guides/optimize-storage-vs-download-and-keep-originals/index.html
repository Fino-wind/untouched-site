Optimize iPhone Storage vs. Download and Keep Originals: what’s the difference?

Updated September 30, 2026

Short answer

Both settings keep your full-resolution originals in iCloud. Optimize iPhone Storage (the default) frees massive storage by keeping smaller preview versions locally and downloading originals on demand, but causes long videos to buffer or fail offline. Download and Keep Originals keeps every file locally for instant offline playback, but requires enough storage to hold your entire library.

When your iPhone lacks space for the entire library but you cannot afford videos buffering, the most effective solution to get the best of both worlds is keeping chosen originals outside the Photos library container—either manually exporting them to the Files app, or preserving them with verified integrity and instant gallery playback using Untouched. The rest of your library stays optimized in iCloud.

Where to change it

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap iCloud, then Photos.
  3. In iOS 27, tap iPhone Storage. In earlier versions the two options are on the Photos screen.
  4. Choose Optimize iPhone Storage or Download and Keep Originals.

Before switching to Download and Keep Originals, check that you have room: Settings › General › iPhone Storage shows free space, and Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Storage shows how much your photo library takes up.

Which should you use? The False Dilemma and The 3 Dead-Ends

Most users believe they are trapped in a strict binary dilemma: either fill their phone completely with Download and Keep Originals, or suffer frequent buffering with Optimize iPhone Storage. Conventional attempts to break this dilemma fail:

  • Dead-End 1: Turn off optimization entirely — Fails if your 150 GB library physically cannot fit on a phone with 20 GB free space.
  • Dead-End 2: Relying on Favorites or "Tap Edit" — Fails because iOS background cache eviction silently purges favorited and recently edited items back to iCloud when storage gets tight.
  • Dead-End 3: Manual Export to Files — Fails on user experience due to silent freezing on batch exports, cryptic IMG_xxxx.MOV filenames, lack of continuous swipe-to-play gallery, and stripped Live Photos.

The Third Way: Hybrid Retention (Untouched)

If your library is larger than your device storage but you need your most important memories (family milestones, travel videos, wedding clips) to always play instantly offline:

  • Keep Optimize iPhone Storage turned on globally to preserve 100+ GB of free space.
  • Use Untouched to pin your chosen videos and photos locally outside the Photos cache container. They stay on your iPhone permanently, play instantly in full HDR, and are never touched by iOS cache eviction. See how to set up hybrid retention.

Common questions

Does Optimize iPhone Storage lower the quality of my photos in iCloud?

No. Apple states that iCloud Photos stores your originals in their original formats at full resolution. Only the versions kept on the iPhone are smaller.

If I switch to Download and Keep Originals, what happens?

Your iPhone starts downloading the originals of items that only had smaller versions on the device. That uses as much space as those originals take, so make sure you have room first.

Can I choose which photos get optimized?

No. The setting applies to your whole library. To guarantee particular originals stay on the device, keep a copy outside the Photos library.

Does tapping “Edit” on a photo prevent it from being offloaded?

No. Tapping Edit forces an immediate download, but iOS cache management will still offload it to iCloud when local storage runs low in the future.

Can I keep my library optimized while keeping 10-20 essential videos offline?

Yes. That is the exact purpose of Untouched: your main library stays optimized in iCloud to save space, while your 10-20 critical videos are pinned locally in an accessible sandboxed gallery for zero-buffering offline playback.
