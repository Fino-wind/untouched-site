What to know before you start

Untouched does one job. Here’s exactly where its edges are.

  • Play the kept copy from Untouched. The Photos app keeps showing its optimized version, as before. The copy that plays instantly lives in Untouched and in Files.
  • It uses space for what you keep. A long 4K video can be several gigabytes. Keep the ones you rewatch; the rest of your library stays slim.
  • Deleting the app deletes its folder. iOS removes an app’s files when you delete it. Copy them out first.
  • A copy isn’t a backup. It’s left out of your iPhone backup by default because the original is already in your library. For a second copy, drag the Untouched folder to a computer or drive now and then.

Questions

Why do my videos take so long to play, or say “Unable to Load Video”?

With Optimize iPhone Storage on, the full video is in iCloud and your iPhone keeps a smaller version. When you play it, Photos downloads the original first. A long video can take minutes on a slow connection, won’t play offline, and sometimes fails with “Unable to Load Video”. Why this happens and what fixes it.

Can I keep one video on my iPhone without turning off Optimize Storage?

Not inside the Photos app: the setting applies to your whole library. That’s exactly what Untouched is for. Pick the video, and Untouched keeps its original file on your iPhone while the rest of your library stays optimized.

Will the video then play instantly in the Photos app?

No. Photos keeps showing its optimized version, just as before. Play the kept copy in Untouched or in Files › On My iPhone › Untouched, where it opens instantly, even offline.

How is this different from “Download and Keep Originals”?

That setting brings every original back onto your iPhone. If your library is 150 GB and your phone was already full, it simply won’t fit. Untouched keeps only the videos and photos you choose, so you keep the space you freed. Compare the two settings.

Does Untouched compress or convert my videos?

No. It copies the original files Photos keeps for each item, byte for byte, and records a SHA-256 checksum for every file so you can verify them later.

How much space does it use?

Exactly the size of what you keep. A few favorite videos might be a few gigabytes, far less than bringing back your whole library.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?

No. There’s no account and no server; your files stay on your iPhone. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, such as whether a copy succeeded and how large it was, and never file names, locations or images. Read the privacy policy.

Why does it need access to all my photos?

The photo picker only tells an app which library item you chose when the app has full access, and that’s the only way to reach the item’s original files. Untouched only reads. It never changes or deletes anything in your library.

Can I keep items from the Hidden album?

Yes. If the Hidden album is locked with Face ID, iOS doesn’t give apps the full item, so Untouched keeps the single file the system hands over. The app marks it “Picker copy”, and it may not be the original. For the complete original, turn off the lock in Settings › Apps › Photos while you add it, then turn it back on.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing you’ve kept is touched. You can still play, verify, export and delete everything. You just can’t add new items beyond the free one.

What happens if I delete the app?

iOS deletes the app’s folder along with it, including everything kept in Untouched. Before deleting the app, copy the folder to another place in Files, to a computer or to a drive.

Does it keep new videos automatically?

No. You choose what to keep, and copying runs while the app is open.

Which devices and languages does it support?

iPhone with iOS {{min_ios}} or later. The app is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

How do I restore a purchase on another iPhone?

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore. Make sure both iPhones are signed in with the same Apple Account.
