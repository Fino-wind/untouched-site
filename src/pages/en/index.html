Why an old photo can look soft on your own phone

With Optimize iPhone Storage turned on, your iPhone keeps full-resolution originals in the cloud and space-saving versions on the device. You see the smaller one first. On a slow connection, or none at all, that’s all you get.

  1. Original on iPhone

    You take the photo

    The full-resolution file is on your iPhone and in your photo library in the cloud.

  2. Smaller copy on iPhone

    Space runs low

    Older photos and videos on the device are swapped for smaller copies. The originals stay in the cloud.

  3. Original downloading…

    You open it later

    The original has to download before you see it sharp. Offline, on a plane, or on weak signal, it doesn’t.

There’s no switch to keep one particular photo on your iPhone. You can keep your whole library, or let the system decide. Untouched adds the missing option: the few you pick.

A copy isn’t a backup

Untouched protects the originals you pick from storage cleanup. It doesn’t protect you from losing the phone. Here’s exactly where the edges are.

  • Deleting the app deletes its folder. iOS removes an app’s files when you delete it. Copy them out first.
  • Left out of iPhone backups by default. These originals usually already exist in your photo library, so Untouched doesn’t add them to your backup twice. You can include them in the app’s Settings.
  • It uses space. Every item is kept at full size. Pick the ones you can’t lose, not everything.
  • Keep a second copy. Every so often, copy the Untouched folder to a computer or a drive. That’s why it’s an ordinary folder.

Questions

Does Untouched compress or convert my photos?

No. It copies the original files Photos keeps for each item, byte for byte, and records a SHA-256 checksum for every file so you can verify them later.

Will it free up space on my iPhone?

No, it uses space. Each item is stored at full size. It’s for the few photos and videos you can’t afford to have reduced, not for your whole library.

How is this different from “Download and Keep Originals”?

That setting keeps every original in your library on your iPhone, which needs room for all of it. Untouched keeps only the items you choose, so the rest of your library can stay optimized. Compare the two settings.

Does it upload my photos anywhere?

No. There’s no account and no server; your files stay on your iPhone. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, such as whether a copy succeeded and how large it was, and never file names, locations or images. Read the privacy policy.

Why does it need access to all my photos?

The photo picker only tells an app which library item you chose when the app has full access, and that’s the only way to reach the item’s original files. Untouched only reads. It never changes or deletes anything in your library.

Can I keep items from the Hidden album?

Yes. If the Hidden album is locked with Face ID, iOS doesn’t give apps the full item, so Untouched keeps the single file the system hands over. The app marks it “Picker copy”, and it may not be the original. For the complete original, turn off the lock in Settings › Apps › Photos while you add it, then turn it back on.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing you’ve kept is touched. You can still open, verify, export and delete everything. You just can’t add new items beyond the free one.

What happens if I delete the app?

iOS deletes the app’s folder along with it, including everything kept in Untouched. Before deleting the app, copy the folder to another place in Files, to a computer or to a drive.

Does it copy new photos automatically?

No. You choose what to keep, and copying runs while the app is open.

Which devices and languages does it support?

iPhone with iOS {{min_ios}} or later. The app is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

How do I restore a purchase on another iPhone?

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore. Make sure both iPhones are signed in with the same Apple Account.
