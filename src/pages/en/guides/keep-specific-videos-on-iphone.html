Short answer

You can’t do it inside the Photos app. Optimize iPhone Storage applies to your whole library; iOS has no setting that exempts one video, one album or your Favorites. What works is keeping a copy of the videos you care about outside the Photos library, where optimization doesn’t reach: export them to the Files app yourself, or use an app such as Untouched (an iPhone app that keeps the original files of the videos and photos you choose on your iPhone). The rest of your library stays optimized, so you keep the space you freed.

Why there’s no per-video switch

Optimize iPhone Storage is one setting for the whole library. With it on, your iPhone decides which originals to replace with smaller versions based on how much space it needs, and Apple offers no way to pin particular items. Anything inside the Photos library is subject to that decision, so the only way to guarantee a video stays playable offline is a copy that isn’t part of the library.

Option 1: Turn off optimization for everything

Choose Download and Keep Originals in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Photos (in iOS 27, then iPhone Storage). Every video plays instantly again.

Trade-off: your iPhone needs room for your entire library. If you turned on Optimize because a 150 GB library had filled a 256 GB phone, it won’t fit back.

Option 2: Export the videos to the Files app yourself

  1. In Photos, select the videos you want to keep on the phone.
  2. Tap Share, then Export Unmodified Original.
  3. Choose a folder under On My iPhone and tap Save.
  4. From then on, play them from the Files app.

If a video’s original is only in iCloud, it downloads first. For edited items, Apple notes that the unmodified original is exported.

Trade-offs: free and built in, but manual. You organize the folder yourself, nothing lets you check later that a file is intact, and videos in a locked Hidden album won’t export until you turn the lock off.

Option 3: Use an app that keeps them for you

Untouched does option 2’s job with the gaps filled in. You pick videos in the usual photo picker. For each one it downloads the original once, then keeps every file Photos holds for the item (a Live Photo’s video, an edited clip’s unedited original) in one readable folder in Files › On My iPhone › Untouched. You play them in Untouched or Files, instantly and offline. It records a SHA-256 checksum for every file so you can verify it later, shows whether each item’s original is still on the device in Photos, and never modifies your library.

Trade-offs: it uses space for what you keep; one item is free and keeping more is paid; and it isn’t a backup. Deleting the app deletes its folder.

If your library fits on your phone, option 1 is simplest. If it doesn’t, and there are a handful of videos you want to be able to play any time, keep those outside the library with option 2 or 3. Either way, copy the ones that matter to a computer or drive now and then: a copy on one device isn’t a backup.
