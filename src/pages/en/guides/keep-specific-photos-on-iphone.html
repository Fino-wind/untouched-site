Short answer

Not inside the Photos app. Optimize iPhone Storage applies to your whole library, and iOS has no setting that exempts one photo, one album or your Favorites. Your choices are to keep every original on the iPhone (Download and Keep Originals), or to keep a copy of the ones you care about outside the Photos library: in the Files app, on a drive, or with an app such as Untouched (an iPhone app that keeps byte-for-byte originals of the photos and videos you choose).

Why there’s no per-photo switch

Optimize iPhone Storage is a single setting for your library. With it on, your iPhone decides which originals to replace with smaller versions based on how much space it needs, and Apple doesn’t offer a way to pin particular items. Anything inside the Photos library is subject to that decision.

So the only way to guarantee a specific original stays on the device is to keep a copy that isn’t part of the Photos library.

Option 1: Keep every original on the iPhone

Choose Download and Keep Originals in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Photos (in iOS 27, then iPhone Storage). Everything stays full size, with no copies to manage.

Trade-off: your iPhone needs room for your entire library. If your library is larger than your free space, this isn’t an option.

Option 2: Export the originals to the Files app yourself

  1. In Photos, select the photos and videos you want to keep.
  2. Tap Share, then Export Unmodified Original.
  3. Choose a folder under On My iPhone, or a connected drive, and tap Save.

If an item’s original is only in iCloud, it downloads first. For edited items, Apple notes that the unmodified original is exported.

Trade-offs: free and built in, but manual. The copies don’t appear in Photos, there’s nothing to check them against later, and items in a locked Hidden album won’t export until you turn the lock off.

Option 3: Use an app that keeps originals for you

Untouched does the same job as option 2 with the gaps filled in. You pick items in the normal photo picker; it copies every original file Photos holds for each one, including a Live Photo’s video and an edited item’s unedited original, into one readable folder per item in Files › On My iPhone › Untouched. It records a SHA-256 checksum for every file so you can confirm later that nothing changed, and it never modifies your photo library.

Trade-offs: it uses space (every item at full size), you keep one item free and pay to keep more, and it isn’t a backup. Deleting the app deletes its folder.

If you have the space, option 1 is simplest. If you don’t, and there are a handful of photos and videos you can’t afford to see reduced, keep those outside the library with option 2 or 3. Either way, copy the ones that matter to a computer or drive now and then: a copy on one device isn’t a backup.
